Questions & Standards

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courier delivery, cake storage rituals, and custom orders.

How are delicate cakes, donuts, and cupcakes kept fresh during delivery?

All orders are transported in custom temperature-controlled refrigerated couriers chilled to precisely 4°C (39°F). Cakes are secured with magnetic velvet bases, and donuts and cupcakes are seated in custom inserts to ensure they arrive in pristine condition.

How much notice is required for Custom Bespoke Celebration Cakes?

For 1 to 2 tier celebration cakes, we recommend 48 to 72 hours of notice. For 3 and 4 tier Royal Architecture cakes with hand-sculpted flowers and 24K gold leaf, we advise booking 1 to 3 weeks in advance.

How should I store and serve the cakes and gourmet donuts?

Our gourmet donuts are best enjoyed on the day of delivery at room temperature. Cakes should be kept refrigerated and brought to room temperature 20 minutes before slicing to allow the buttercream to soften to velvety perfection.

Do you cater for nut allergies and vegetarian preferences?

Yes. All of our cakes, donuts, and cupcakes are 100% vegetarian. We also have clearly labeled nut-free creations, such as our Bourbon Vanilla Bean Cupcakes and Royal Red Velvet Cake. You can filter our menu by dietary tags at any time.
